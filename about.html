01 The story

A designer obsessed with measurable clarity

I'm Khushal Ohlyan, a UI/UX designer based in Gurugram, India. I design end-to-end user journeys and scalable design systems, with measurable impact on conversion, engagement and delivery efficiency. I started out in architecture, which is where I first learned to design around how people actually behave.

My first product role was at Square Yards, where I designed SQ Beats, an end-to-end interior design platform with VR property walkthroughs that helped lift sales conversions by 30%. At Primeval Technologies I designed wink.finance, a consumer DeFi platform, simplifying onboarding and design handoff.

Wanting deeper rigor, I completed an MSc in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Birmingham, running eye-tracking and EEG studies and moderating 25+ usability sessions with 100+ participants. Since then I've designed a supplier-diversity portal at GoDiverse, and next-generation in-vehicle experiences for two future Jaguar Land Rover EV programs.

The thread through all of it: understand the human, structure the journey, and build systems that scale without losing clarity.

02 How I work

Four rules I don't compromise on

Proven across four industries.

01

Research before pixels

Every project starts with evidence: interviews, telemetry, or a heuristic teardown. Never with assumptions. If we don't understand the behavior, we're decorating, not designing.

02

Systems, not screens

Component libraries, design tokens and documentation, so consistency doesn't depend on me being in the room. My libraries have cut handoff time by 25%.

03

Measure the impact

-22% onboarding drop-off, +30% conversions, +400% engagement. Design should move numbers, and I hold my work to that standard.

04

Accessible by default

WCAG 2.1 AA isn't a checkbox at the end. It's how the work stays usable for everyone, from enterprise buyers to drivers glancing at a console.
